Two Windows, One Throw

How much time did the offense have with at least one of its two wide receivers in space before the throw? Two real 2021 plays, measured over the last second before release.

How it is measured

Passing lanes, pass rush pressure, and the quarterback's reads are outside the measurement. It describes the options the two receivers created, not where the ball went: in both plays shown, the pass went to a tight end.
